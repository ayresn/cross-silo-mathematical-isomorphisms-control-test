```math
\frac{d\mathbf{p}}{dt} = q(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})
```
where \(\mathbf{p}\) is the momentum, \(q\) is the charge, \(\mathbf{E}\) is the electric field, and \(\mathbf{B}\) is the magnetic field.
```math
\frac{d\mathbf{S}}{dt} = \mathbf{S} \times \mathbf{H}_{\text{eff}}
```
where \(\mathbf{S}\) is the spin vector and \(\mathbf{H}_{\text{eff}}\) is the effective magnetic field. Both systems exhibit Hamiltonian dynamics with similar mathematical structures.
